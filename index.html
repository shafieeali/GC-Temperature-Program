Illustrative retention model for a 30 m PDMS capillary column with a dead time of 1.0 min and about 10,000 plates. Each alkane’s retention factor follows ln k = ΔH/RT + constant, with ΔH rising by about 4.8 kJ/mol per CH₂ group. The model reproduces the answer key: at 150 °C, C16 elutes at about 48 min, and with the 35 → 280 °C program, C21 leaves at about 262 °C. Every alkane has the same peak area, so narrower peaks are taller. Very sharp peaks are cut off at the top of the chart.
